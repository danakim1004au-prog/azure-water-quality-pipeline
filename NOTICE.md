## Data sources

The pipeline is designed around public, openly-licensed South Australian data
sources. When their data is ingested, attribution to the originating agencies is
required under the respective licences:

| Source | Provider | Licence |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **WaterConnect** (groundwater levels & salinity) | Government of South Australia (DEW) | Creative Commons Attribution |
| **SILO** (gridded climate / rainfall) | Queensland Government / Bureau of Meteorology | Creative Commons Attribution / Open |
| **water.data.sa.gov.au** (surface-water levels) | Government of South Australia | Open |

The dataset shipped in this repository is **synthetic demonstration data**
generated by [`scripts/generate_sample_data.py`](scripts/generate_sample_data.py);
it carries no third-party licence and contains no real readings.
